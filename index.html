Who this is for

Small sellers based outside the EU (US, UK, Canada, Australia and elsewhere) who sell physical products to EU or Northern Ireland buyers through Etsy, Shopify, eBay or Amazon. That includes handmade sellers, resellers and small brands.

If you're based in the EU, the template pack still applies, but you won't need an EU Responsible Person.

Since 13 December 2024, the EU's General Product Safety Regulation (GPSR, Regulation (EU) 2023/988) requires online listings shown to EU consumers to include manufacturer details, an EU-based responsible person for non-EU manufacturers, product identification, and any warnings in the buyer's language. Marketplaces can hide listings that don't have this information.

FAQ

Will this make my shop "compliant"?

No download can promise that. The templates are based on the regulation and the marketplaces' help pages. You fill them in, and you stay responsible for your products and what you publish.

Do I really need an EU Responsible Person?

If you're a manufacturer outside the EU and there's no EU importer or other EU economic operator for your product, Article 4 of Regulation (EU) 2019/1020, as applied by GPSR Article 16, generally means one is required. The guide walks you through how to check. We don't provide one.

What about packaging / EPR?

Several EU countries require separate packaging registration (for example Germany's LUCID register and France's unique EPR ID), and marketplaces may hide listings without those numbers. The pack points you to the official registers. Fees go to those schemes.

Are the translations reliable?

They're starting points and haven't been checked by native speakers yet. Have a native speaker or a second translation check safety wording before you publish.
